CUDA-Q Community
****************

The CUDA-Q ecosystem is extended by projects built and maintained by the
community. This page lists a curated selection of them, each with documentation
and a runnable example.

.. note::

   Community projects are developed and maintained independently of NVIDIA.
   They are listed for convenience and are not part of CUDA-Q or supported by
   the CUDA-Q team. Please direct questions and issues to each project's own
   repository.
